# Inkvoice Trademark & Brand Use Policy

**The short version:** The Inkvoice *source code* is free and open under the
[MIT License](./LICENSE). The Inkvoice *name and logos* are not — they are
trademarks. You can do almost anything you like with the code; this page only
covers the few things that involve the **brand**.

We keep this policy because the name and logo are how people recognize the
official Inkvoice. The MIT License grants rights to the **code** and
deliberately does not grant trademark rights. This page explains what is fine
to do without asking, and what needs a quick word with us first.

## What this covers

- The word mark **"Inkvoice"** (and confusingly similar variants).
- The Inkvoice logos, marks, and lockups, including the files in
  [`docs/branding/`](./docs/branding).

Inkvoice™ and the Inkvoice logos are trademarks of **Barış Çelik** ("the
maintainer").

## You can do these freely — no permission needed

- Run, modify, fork, and redistribute the **code** under the MIT License.
- Use the name **to refer to the project** truthfully — "built on Inkvoice",
  "compatible with Inkvoice", "this server runs Inkvoice", tutorials, reviews,
  and comparisons.
- Redistribute **unmodified** Inkvoice keeping its existing branding.
- Link to the project with a **"Powered by Inkvoice"** credit.

## Please ask first — we're friendly, and it's usually a yes

- Using the **Inkvoice name or logo as the brand of a product or paid service**
  in a way that implies it is official or endorsed by us.
- Offering a **commercial hosted or managed "Inkvoice" service** and presenting
  it as official, recommended, or affiliated. *(Hosting unmodified Inkvoice and
  describing it factually — e.g. "Inkvoice hosting" — is fine. The line is
  implying endorsement. If you are a hosting provider and would like an
  official or endorsed listing, get in touch — we would love to partner.)*
- Distributing a **modified fork under the Inkvoice name.** Please give your
  fork its own name; you can still say it is "based on Inkvoice."
- **Merchandise**, or domain names, using the Inkvoice name or logo.

## Please don't

- Imply official status, endorsement, partnership, or affiliation that does not
  exist.
- Alter, recolor, or redraw the logo, or combine it with other marks.
- Register trademarks, company names, or domains that are confusingly similar
  to Inkvoice.
